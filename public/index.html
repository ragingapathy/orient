About & privacy

Map tiles load from OpenFreeMap. Numbered U.S. addresses go to the U.S. Census geocoder; place-name searches go to Photon. Searches are sent only when you press Find address. Nearby discovery sends the chosen map coordinates to Overpass only when you request it or tap a map place. Website reading sends only the website address you choose to the local reader, which requests public pages directly. Saved marks and notes stay in this browser and on the computer that runs Orient.

Calendar entries are private and editable. Community threads are demo content. Personal replies are local drafts, visible only to you.
